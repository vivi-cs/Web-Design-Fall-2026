Academic Excellence

Education that goes beyond the classroom.

At SUNY New Paltz, learning is an active experience. Students work closely with faculty, explore new ideas, and apply what they learn to real-world challenges.

With programs spanning the arts, sciences, business, education, technology, and liberal arts, you can build an education around the questions and subjects that inspire you.

Small classes, collaborative projects, research, internships, and experiential learning help prepare students for meaningful careers and lifelong learning.

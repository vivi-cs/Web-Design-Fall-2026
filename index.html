Why New Paltz?

A college experience built around you.

At SUNY New Paltz, students are encouraged to ask questions, challenge assumptions, and turn their ideas into action.

Located in the heart of the Hudson Valley, our campus combines a strong academic foundation with opportunities for research, creativity, leadership, and community engagement.

Whether you're discovering a new passion or preparing for your future career, you'll find people and programs ready to help you make your mark.
